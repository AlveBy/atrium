- Own tabs for custom fields. An administrator presses **Tabs** on a module's list, adds a tab (a name in every content language, a code, a sort number, optionally one site) and picks it in the **Tab** of a field. The tab is drawn in the module's record form at its place by the sort number (a form whose tabs have no numbers is numbered 0, 10, 20 … in its order), only while it has fields; renaming keeps the fields on it, and deleting it moves its fields to the default tab with their values kept. A module whose form has no tabs gets them with its first added tab (its own fields stay together on a first one), for any tab added through `atrium.module.tabs`. The tabs have their own permissions (`custom_field_tabs.*`) and labels in 15 languages ([docs/custom-fields.md](docs/custom-fields.md)).
