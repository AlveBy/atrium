- Scheduled publishing for every content module ([docs/history.md](docs/history.md#scheduled-publishing)), with no code of the module's own. A form with an **Active** field gets **Publish at** and **Unpublish at** next to it, on its tab: at the first time the record switches itself on, at the second off; the end must come after the start, and emptying both cancels the plan. The times are kept in `admin_schedules`, so a module needs no column for them, and the form, `/api/v1` (`publish_at`, `unpublish_at`), MCP, an import and an export take them the same way. The list marks a planned record **Scheduled** with a clock next to its **Active** column, the tooltip saying when. `atrium:publish` runs every minute and switches what is due through the record's form, so the activity log writes it **by the scheduler** on behalf of whoever planned it, a version is kept and caches and the search follow; when both times have come the record ends switched off, a switch the module refuses is reported once, a record in the trash waits and one deleted for good takes its plan along. `usesAdminSchedule(): false` turns it off for one model; service modules have none. An export now writes a date a package keeps outside the record's table too. Labels in 15 languages.
