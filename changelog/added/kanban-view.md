- A **kanban board** for any module's list ([docs/modules.md](docs/modules.md#kanban-board)): a module returns `KanbanView::make('status')->title('name')->cards('rating', 'posted_at')->order('sort')` from `getAdministrationViews()` and its list gets a **Table / Kanban** switch (remembered per administrator and module). The columns are the options of a select field or the records of a relation, plus a "No value" column while it has cards; a card shows the title and up to three fields and opens the record. Dragging a card to another column (or its **Move to column** select) saves it through the form's own save path, with its validation, hooks, site and permission checks, and with `order()` the place inside the column too; without `update` nothing moves. Columns load 20 cards and **Show more** loads the rest; the board follows the table's search, filters and site. Reviews (by status) and FAQ questions (by category, in order) have one; the drag library loads in a separate file only where a board is shown. Labels in 15 languages.
