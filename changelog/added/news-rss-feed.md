- An RSS 2.0 feed of the news: `/news.xml` and `/de/news.xml` (the news prefix and `.xml`, in each language and for a site in a folder too). It lists the latest active, already published news of the current site written in that language, newest first, with an absolute link, a publication date, the preview text as plain text and the preview picture as an enclosure; text is escaped and characters XML forbids are dropped. **Modules → News → Settings** sets how many items (1 to 100, 20 by default, per site). The public theme adds `<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml">` to the head of the news pages through the `atrium.head` filter. Labels in 15 languages. See [docs/feeds.md](docs/feeds.md).
