- Tracker module (`alveby/atrium-tracker`, `atrium:module:install alveby/atrium-tracker`, [docs/tracker.md](docs/tracker.md)): tasks for the people who work in the admin, with no client cabinet. Projects with a key (`SHOP-1`, `SHOP-2` …), a lead and members; tasks of four types (task, story, bug, epic) with a status, a priority shown by an arrow and a word, an assignee, a parent task (subtasks, the tasks of an epic), labels, a due date, an estimate in hours and attachments; a **Comments and history** tab with the comments and a line for every change of status, assignee and priority; a **My tasks** button and filters by project, status, assignee, type, label, overdue and mine. The assignee gets a letter, in their own language, when a task is given to them and when its status changes; the statuses and the letters are set on the module's settings screen. Two actions, `atrium.tracker.task.status_changed` and `atrium.tracker.task.assigned`, for other modules. With `--demo` it is filled with two projects and ten tasks. Labels in 15 languages.
