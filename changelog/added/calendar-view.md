- A **calendar** for any module's list ([docs/modules.md](docs/modules.md#calendar)): a module returns `CalendarView::make('active_from')->title('name')` (optionally `->until('ends_at')`) from `getAdministrationViews()` and its list gets a **Calendar** button next to **Table** and **Kanban**, with month, week and list modes. A record dragged to another day is saved the way the form saves it (`PATCH {module}/{id}/move`: validation, hooks, site and `update` permission, the time of day and the length are kept); a click on a day opens the create form with the day filled in; the search and the filters work as in the table. Demo: News by the date they go live. Nothing changes in the table, the form or `/api/v1`.
