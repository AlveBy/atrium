- `alveby/atrium-1c`, the 1C connector ([docs/1c.md](docs/1c.md)): 1C:Enterprise sends its catalogue over the standard CommerceML 2 exchange (`type=catalog`: login, init, files in chunks and in zip, import in steps) to `/atrium-1c/exchange` or to the old Bitrix address `/bitrix/admin/1c_exchange.php`, so a stock configuration changes only the site address, the login and the password. Sections, products, features, pictures, prices and in stock / not in stock go into the catalogue and are matched by the 1C identifier, so a repeated exchange updates instead of duplicating; a deleted product is switched off. **Modules → 1C** takes the login and the password (the password is stored encrypted and shown as a mask); **Modules → 1C exchange** lists the runs with what came, what was skipped and the errors, and every exchange is a line of the exchange log. 20 wrong logins a minute from one address are refused until the minute is over. Orders to 1C (`type=sale`) wait for the shop: 1C is answered that there are none. Labels in 15 languages.
