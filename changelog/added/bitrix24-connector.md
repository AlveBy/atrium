- `alveby/atrium-bitrix24`, the Bitrix24 connector ([docs/bitrix24.md](docs/bitrix24.md)): a form submission becomes a lead, or a contact with a deal, sent from the queue after it is stored, with five attempts and a line of the exchange log each; **Modules → Bitrix24** takes the incoming webhook address (stored encrypted, shown as a mask, hidden in the log), a switch for sending forms, the code of the Bitrix24 chat widget that is added to every page of the site, and a **Check connection** button that names the portal and the user; **Bitrix24: forms** chooses lead or deal per form, switches a form off and maps form fields to Bitrix24 fields line by line (fields without a line go by their type, the rest into the comment). Orders, customers and deal stages coming back wait for the shop. Labels in 15 languages.
