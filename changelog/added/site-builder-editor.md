- A page editor for pages made of blocks: blocks are dragged between columns and sections or moved with buttons from the keyboard, the settings of the selected block stand beside the page, the theme draws a live preview on the server, and a page can be kept as a draft, published, or taken back to an earlier version ([the guide](docs/site-builder.md#what-an-editor-does)). A saved page's blocks are now edited there, not on its form. Any record with a `LayoutField` gets the editor; a model gives it a preview by implementing `Previewable`. Needs `php artisan migrate`.
