- An activity log of every module ([docs/history.md](docs/history.md)), with no code of the module's own. Who created, changed or deleted a record, when, and from where: the panel, `/api/v1` and MCP (the token's owner), an import (whoever started it), a module's button or bulk action (whoever pressed it, also on the queue), a command. A change lists the fields that differ, from what to what, as the form shows them (translations by language, relation fields by ids, custom and SEO fields); a save that changes nothing leaves no entry, and the entry is written in the record's transaction. Passwords, hidden attributes and fields named like `password`, `secret`, `token`, `api_key` (`atrium.history.secrets`) are written as changed, never with a value. **Activity log** in the menu narrows by module, administrator and days, 50 entries a page, only of the modules the administrator may view and of the current site; every record form has a **History** button. `ATRIUM_HISTORY_ENABLED=false` turns it off, `hasAdminHistory(): false` turns it off for one model; `ActivityLog::watch()`, `during()` and `record()` serve code that writes records its own way. Labels in 15 languages; the old and the new value differ by a strike-through and by the column's name, not only by colour.
