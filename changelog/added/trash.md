- A trash for every content module ([docs/history.md](docs/history.md#trash)), with no code of the module's own. A deleted record, from the list, a bulk delete, `/api/v1` or MCP, is hidden everywhere (panel, site, search, sitemap, API) and kept in its table with its files, SEO, links, versions and child records; service modules (administrators, languages, form submissions, logs) still delete at once, and `usesAdminTrash(): false` makes any module do so. **Trash** in the menu lists what was deleted, by whom and when, narrowed by module and limited to the modules the administrator may view and to the current site; **Restore** brings a record back and opens it, **Delete for good** deletes it with its files and versions after a confirmation, and both go to the activity log. Two permissions per module, `{module}.restore` and `{module}.force_delete`. The scheduler deletes for good what has been in the trash longer than `ATRIUM_TRASH_DAYS` (30; 0 keeps everything) with `atrium:trash:empty`, keeping what a module refuses to delete. `GET /api/v1/{module}/trash`, `POST …/trash/{id}/restore` and `DELETE …/trash/{id}`, and the MCP tools `list_trash`, `restore_from_trash` and `delete_forever`, do the same. Review counters and the search rebuild leave the trash out. Labels in 15 languages.
