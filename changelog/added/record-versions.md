- Versions of every record ([docs/history.md](docs/history.md#versions)), with no code of the module's own. Every save, from the form, `/api/v1`, MCP or an import, keeps what the form held after it, and the first save of an older record keeps it as it was before. A record's **History** lists the versions with when, who and from where, **Compare** shows two of them field by field, and **Restore** puts an older one back through the module's form, validated and logged, after a confirmation and with `{module}.update`; a version the form no longer takes is refused with the reason. The newest 50 a record are kept (`ATRIUM_HISTORY_REVISIONS`, 0 keeps none), no password or key is in them, and a record deleted for good takes them along. `GET /api/v1/{module}/{id}/versions` and `POST …/versions/{version}/restore`, and the MCP tools `list_versions` and `restore_version`, do the same. `hasAdminRevisions(): false` turns them off for one model; administrators keep none. Labels in 15 languages.
