- A Yandex Market feed (YML, `yml_catalog`) of the catalog: `/catalog.xml` and `/de/catalog.xml` (the catalog prefix and `.xml`). The shop block (name, company, url, currency), the active sections as categories with their parents, and an offer for every product that is on sale now and has a price, a section on the site and a page: address, price, currency, up to ten pictures, name, description, availability from "In stock", the brand as the vendor when the Brands module is installed, the article as the vendor code. Written as it is read, in chunks, so a large catalog needs no memory; text is escaped and characters XML forbids are dropped. **Modules → Catalog → Settings** sets the shop name (up to 20 characters), the company and the currency of the prices (RUB, BYN, KZT, UAH, USD or EUR), per site. Labels in 15 languages. See [docs/feeds.md](docs/feeds.md).
