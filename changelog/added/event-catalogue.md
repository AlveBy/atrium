- The event catalogue, the first part of the extension platform ([docs/extensions.md](docs/extensions.md)). A module declares what its hooks mean to the outside with `Hook::register('atrium.events', 'shop.order.paid', Event::make('atrium.order.paid')->label(…)->subject(Order::class)->payload(fn (Order $order): array => […]))`, and every module of the panel has `{module}.created`, `{module}.updated` and `{module}.deleted` without a line of its own. When a declared hook fires, `atrium.event.occurred` is fired with an `Occurrence`: an id, the key, the record and its module, the payload, the administrator who did it, the site and the time, and `toArray()` gives the JSON a webhook will send. Nothing is built while nothing listens. `php artisan atrium:events` lists the catalogue. Labels in 15 languages. See "The event catalogue" in [docs/modules.md](docs/modules.md).
